## Everything here deserves a second reading

This blog was born from the desire to record what I learn along the way. Not as a final manual, but as a public notebook where ideas, discoveries, questions, and experiments are stored for future revisits.

Most of the content here exists for a simple reason: I believe knowledge that is never revisited tends to fade away. Writing is how I organize my thoughts, fix concepts in place, and turn experiences into something useful, both for myself and for whoever finds these pages along the way.

You will find technical notes, studies, interface experiments, reflections on software development, and occasionally some digressions about technology and learning.

In the end, this blog is a record of my growth as a developer, a place to return to years from now and remember not only what I learned, but how I thought while learning it.
